Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Peer-Reviewed Parenting ("we," "us") handles information on this website and in the private publishing tool we use to post our own content to social media platforms.

This website

This website does not collect personal information from visitors. It has no sign-up forms, no comments, no cookies set by us, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Our publishing tool

We use a private, internal tool to publish Peer-Reviewed Parenting's own videos and images to our own accounts through the official APIs of YouTube (Google), Facebook and Instagram (Meta), and Pinterest. The tool is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

  • What it accesses: only the Peer-Reviewed Parenting accounts that the owner signs in with and approves. It uses that access to upload and schedule our own posts, update their titles, descriptions, captions, and boards, and read basic performance statistics (such as views) for our own content.
  • What it does not access: it does not read, collect, or store personal information about other people, including viewers, followers, or commenters, beyond what appears in our own account's aggregate statistics.
  • Sign-in tokens: the access tokens the platforms issue when the owner approves the tool are stored securely on a private server with restricted access. They are used only to operate our own accounts.
  • No sharing or selling: we do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through these platforms with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Our use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Other platforms

Use of Facebook and Instagram is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy, and use of Pinterest is subject to Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account → Third-party apps & services
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations, or Instagram Settings → Apps and websites
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps

When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.

Children

Our content is about parenting and is meant for adults. This website does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
